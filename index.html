India’s Future Runs on Silicon

An Open Letter to Indian Tech Startups: From Software to Semiconductors

Too Much Code, Too Little Core

Indian startups have made huge strides in apps and services — but it's time to go deeper. We import over 90% of our semiconductors. That’s both a weakness and an opportunity.

Chips Are the New Gold

AI, EVs, 5G, even space missions — they all rely on chips. If India wants to lead the future, we must own the silicon layer.

Dear Indian Founders,

The next wave of innovation won’t come from faster deliveries — it’ll come from faster processors. From fabs to fabs, chips are the beating heart of the digital world.

If India wants to lead in AI, EVs, or space — we need to own the silicon stack. This is a national mission, and we need YOU to step up.

Build chip design startups. Build tools that support semiconductor R&D. Push for India's own fab ecosystem. Be the builder of tomorrow’s India — etched in silicon.

Sincerely,
Pushkarini
